## 2024-05-24 - Missing titles in admonitions
**Learning:** Found several `!!! note` blocks in use-guides missing a title (e.g. `!!! note "Title"`), leading to inconsistent look compared to other admonitions which use `!!! note "Didactic guide"`, `!!! note "Machine accounts"`, etc.
**Action:** Always add a concise, relevant title to admonition blocks when writing MkDocs Material markdown.
