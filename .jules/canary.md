## 2024-07-20 - Convert Blockquotes to Admonitions
**Learning:** Legacy markdown blockquotes with bold text (e.g. `> **Note:**`) are harder to notice and scan than MkDocs Material admonitions.
**Action:** Convert standard markdown blockquotes in guides into Material admonitions (e.g. `!!! note`) using 4-space indentation for the body text to improve visual hierarchy.
